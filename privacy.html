Key Summary for Players: CubeFit works offline without requiring account registration. Game progress, coins, unlocked cosmetics, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Analytics and advertising partners initialize only after you grant consent via our in-app GDPR prompt.

1. Data Controller & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Tunevo ("Tunevo", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit our website at https://tunevo.si or play our mobile application CubeFit (Android package / iOS bundle ID: com.tunevo.cubefit).

Data Controller Contact: Tunevo — Email: privacy@tunevo.si | Support: support@tunevo.si.

2. Information We Collect & Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

CubeFit does not require you to create an account or submit your name, password, or phone number. To deliver gameplay, CubeFit stores the following data strictly on your device:

  • Game Progress: Completed levels, stars earned, coin balance, hint stocks (Hint, Compass, Magnet, Time Freeze), and customizer unlocks.
  • App Preferences: Music/SFX mute states, haptic feedback toggle, language choice (EN/FR), left-handed mode, rotation control mode, and reduced-motion preference.
  • Consent Record: Your in-app GDPR/privacy consent status (LKL_GDPR_ConsentAccepted).

B. Technical, Diagnostic & Advertising Data (Only With Consent Where Required)

When you opt in or interact with optional features (such as rewarded video ads or analytics), our integrated SDKs may automatically process:

  • Device & App Info: Device model, OS version, screen resolution, app version, language, and approximate region/country inferred from IP address (IP is not stored in raw form by Tunevo).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android or Apple IDFA on iOS (subject to App Tracking Transparency / ATT permission and GDPR consent).
  • In-App Purchase (IAP) Transaction Data: Product SKU, purchase confirmation tokens, and entitlement status (e.g., No-Ads bundle, Coin packs). Payment card details are processed solely by Google Play or the Apple App Store and are never shared with Tunevo.

C. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you email support@tunevo.si or privacy@tunevo.si, we receive your email address, device details, and message content solely to resolve your inquiry.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We only work with established infrastructure, store, and monetization partners compliant with international privacy standards:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Engine, Unity Ads & Analytics): Used for 3D rendering, optional rewarded video ads, and stability telemetry. Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob (when enabled): Used for Android app distribution, Google Play Billing, and user messaging/UMP consent. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store & StoreKit: Used for iOS app distribution and in-app purchase validation. Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

CubeFit is designed as an accessible, family-friendly spatial puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable digital age of consent in the EEA/UK, typically 13–16) without verifiable parental consent. Where a user indicates they are below the age threshold, personalized behavioral advertising is disabled and only contextual, privacy-safe operation applies. Parents or guardians who believe data was collected inadvertently may contact privacy@tunevo.si for immediate erasure.

6. Data Retention, Transfers & Security

  • Retention: Local saves remain on your device until you tap Settings > Reset Progress or uninstall CubeFit. Support correspondence is deleted within 24 months after ticket resolution unless required by tax or legal obligations.
  • International Transfers: Our partners may process diagnostic or advertising tokens on servers outside the EEA using European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent safeguards.
  • Security: Our website enforces HTTPS/TLS encryption, and CubeFit limits network calls exclusively to verified Store and consented SDK endpoints.

7. Your Privacy Rights (EEA/UK GDPR & US State Laws)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access, Rectify & Port Data: Request confirmation of any personal data held about you.
  • Erase Data ("Right to be Forgotten"): Instantly wipe local save data via CubeFit's Settings or submit a formal erasure request via our Data Deletion Portal.
  • Withdraw Consent & Opt Out: Manage tracking via iOS App Tracking Transparency, Android Privacy Settings ("Delete Advertising ID"), or our GDPR Center.
  • Lodge a Complaint: EU residents may lodge a complaint with their local Data Protection Authority (e.g., the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia — Informacijski pooblaščenec, or CNIL in France).

8. Contact & Policy Changes

For any privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact Tunevo at privacy@tunevo.si. We may update this Privacy Policy as CubeFit evolves; the effective date at the top always reflects the latest version.

2. Données Collectées et Traitées

A. Données stockées localement sur votre appareil

CubeFit ne requiert ni inscription, ni nom, ni mot de passe. L'application sauvegarde localement :

  • Progression de jeu : niveaux terminés, étoiles, solde de pièces, stocks d'indices (Indice, Boussole, Aimant, Gel du Temps) et personnalisations du cactus.
  • Préférences : volumes musique/effets, vibrations, langue (FR/EN), mode gaucher, mode de rotation et animations réduites.
  • Preuve de consentement : votre choix RGPD enregistré localement (LKL_GDPR_ConsentAccepted).

B. Données techniques et publicitaires (uniquement avec votre accord le cas échéant)

  • Informations techniques : modèle d'appareil, version Android/iOS, langue système, résolution d'écran et pays approximatif.
  • Identifiants publicitaires : GAID (Android) ou IDFA (iOS), sous réserve de votre consentement RGPD et de l'autorisation Apple ATT.
  • Achats intégrés (IAP) : références des packs achetés (ex. Pack Sans Pub) et jetons de validation fournis par Google Play ou Apple App Store. Tunevo n'a jamais accès à vos données bancaires.

C. Données fournies volontairement

Si vous contactez support@tunevo.si ou privacy@tunevo.si, nous utilisons votre adresse e-mail et votre message uniquement pour vous répondre.

  • Sécurité : le site tunevo.si utilise le chiffrement HTTPS/TLS et CubeFit restreint ses appels réseau aux API officielles des stores et aux SDK autorisés.

Conformément au RGPD (Articles 15 à 22), vous disposez d'un droit d'accès, de rectification, d'effacement, de limitation, d'opposition et de portabilité, ainsi que du droit de retirer votre consentement à tout moment.

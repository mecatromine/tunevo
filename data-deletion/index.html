GOOGLE PLAY DATA SAFETY & GDPR ART. 17

Data & Progress Deletion

How to permanently delete your local CubeFit save data or request erasure of advertising and diagnostic identifiers.

Steps

  • 1. Instant In-App Deletion
  • 2. What Gets Deleted
  • 3. Formal Erasure Request
No Cloud Accounts Created: CubeFit (com.tunevo.cubefit) operates offline without user accounts, logins, or Tunevo-hosted player profile servers. Your game progression is stored directly on your mobile device.

1. Instant Self-Service Deletion

  1. Erase Game Progress Inside CubeFit: Open CubeFit → tap the Settings gear icon → scroll to Support & Legal → tap Reset Progress and confirm. This immediately deletes completed levels, coins, stars, and unlocks.
  2. Remove All Local Preferences & Cache: Uninstall CubeFit from your Android or iOS device (or clear App Storage in Android Settings) to permanently remove all local PlayerPrefs and consent flags.
  3. Reset Device Advertising ID:
    • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete Advertising ID.
    • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off CubeFit.

2. Data Categories & Retention Summary

Data Type Storage Location Retention / Deletion
Levels, stars, coins, boosters, cactus customizations Local device storage only Deleted immediately via Reset Progress or uninstall
Audio, handedness, rotation & language settings Local device PlayerPrefs Deleted immediately upon uninstalling the app
Store purchase receipts (No-Ads, Packs) Google Play / Apple Account Managed by Google/Apple; non-consumables remain restorable under store rules
Support emails & consented SDK IDs (IDFA/GAID) Tunevo Support / Third-party SDKs Erased within 30 days upon verified request below
Aucun compte cloud : CubeFit (com.tunevo.cubefit) fonctionne sans création de compte ni serveur de profil Tunevo. Votre sauvegarde est stockée localement sur votre appareil.

1. Suppression Immédiate en Autonomie

  1. Effacer la progression dans CubeFit : Ouvrez CubeFit → touchez l'icône Paramètres → descendez jusqu'à Support et Légal → touchez Réinitialiser ma progression et confirmez.
  2. Supprimer toutes les préférences locales : Désinstallez CubeFit (ou effacez les données de l'application sur Android) pour détruire immédiatement toutes les préférences locales et clés de consentement.
  3. Supprimer l'identifiant publicitaire :
    • Android : Paramètres → Confidentialité → Annonces → Supprimer l'identifiant publicitaire.
    • iOS : Réglages → Confidentialité et sécurité → Suivi → désactivez CubeFit.

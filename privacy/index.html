Summary: CubeFit works without requiring account creation. Game progress and preferences stay locally on your Android device. Optional analytics and ad partners only initialize after your consent.

1. Data Controller & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how tunevo ("tunevo", "we", "us") handles information when you use our website or play CubeFit (com.tunevo.cubefit) distributed on Google Play.

Privacy Contact: privacy@tunevo.si | Support: support@tunevo.si.

2. Information Collected & Processed

A. Stored Locally on Your Device

CubeFit does not ask you to register an account, username, or password. Level progress, stars, coins, booster counts, customizer unlocks, language, audio, and consent preferences remain locally on your device.

B. Technical, Store & Advertising Data

  • Device Diagnostics: Basic device model, Android OS version, screen size, and crash reports to maintain app stability.
  • Advertising Identifier (GAID): Processed by advertising partners solely when you grant consent in our in-app GDPR screen (and subject to Android privacy settings).
  • Google Play Purchases: In-app purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We receive purchase verification tokens and product identifiers only—never your payment details.

C. Support Inquiries

If you email support@tunevo.si or privacy@tunevo.si, we process your email address and message strictly to assist you.

4. Service Providers

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: App distribution, Google Play Billing, consent messaging (UMP), and ads. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies: Application runtime, stability diagnostics, and rewarded video ads. Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

We comply with COPPA and GDPR-K requirements and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the applicable age of digital consent without parental authorization. Parents may write to privacy@tunevo.si for prompt deletion.

6. Retention & Security

Local saves stay on your device until you reset progress inside CubeFit Settings or uninstall the app. Support correspondence is deleted within 24 months of resolution unless legally required otherwise.

7. Your GDPR & Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent at any time, or request erasure via our Data Deletion page or GDPR Center. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact privacy@tunevo.si.

2. Données Traitées

A. Données stockées localement

CubeFit ne requiert ni inscription ni mot de passe. Niveaux, étoiles, pièces, bonus, personnalisation du cactus, langue et préférences restent stockés sur votre appareil.

B. Données techniques, achats et publicité

  • Diagnostics techniques : modèle d'appareil, version Android et journaux d'erreur pour assurer la stabilité.
  • Identifiant publicitaire (GAID) : traité uniquement si vous accordez votre consentement RGPD dans l'application.
  • Achats Google Play : gérés exclusivement par Google Play Billing. tunevo ne reçoit jamais vos coordonnées bancaires.

C. Messages de support

Si vous écrivez à support@tunevo.si ou privacy@tunevo.si, vos messages servent uniquement à traiter votre demande.
